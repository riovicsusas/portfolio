E-commerce Systems Analyst

I connect the moving parts of e-commerce.

I bring an electronics engineering foundation and customer-support experience to the way I approach store operations. I’m interested in what happens between the storefront, the warehouse, and the support team—and how to make those handoffs clearer.

My portfolio explores order exceptions, fulfillment routing, catalog data, and automation. I trace problems back to their source, map the workflow, and develop practical controls and documentation. My aim is to help teams understand what happened, decide what to do next, and maintain the systems they rely on.
