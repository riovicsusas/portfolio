Synapse Botanicals — Enterprise Operational Architecture

Synapse Botanicals is an engineered sandbox for modeling the operations of a direct-to-consumer health and wellness brand. Built around a Shopify Plus reference architecture, its case studies explore distributed fulfillment, data integrity, and asynchronous automation through controlled simulations and reproducible local tests.

Product Catalog Matrix

12 Master Variants across 5 product lines (Focus Stick Packs, Adaptogen Tinctures, Starter Bundles, Precision Tumblers, and Whisks). High average unit volume requiring strict lot controls, expiration tracking, and multi-node routing.

Order Volume Scenario

800–1,200 orders/day is the modeled peak workload. Customer address changes and discount combinations provide controlled test cases for automated triage.

Multi-Node Fulfillment

Modeled fulfillment network spanning Allentown, PA and Reno, NV, used to test inventory-aware routing and Shopify-to-WMS coordination.
