Order-to-Fulfillment Systems Case Studies

Three focused studies examine order-state control, multi-node routing, and the failure handoffs between commerce and fulfillment systems.

Shared reference scenario

Synapse Botanicals is a fictional direct-to-consumer store used to make the examples concrete. Its studies model Shopify order state, fulfillment routing between Allentown and Reno, and cross-system recovery. Orders, rates, and timings are declared scenarios, not records from a live merchant.
