With a foundation in Electronics Engineering (Finite State Machines, Logic Systems, and Signal Telemetry) paired with frontline logistics triage at Amazon Operation Services, I specialize in diagnosing and hardening the critical failure seams between e-commerce platforms and distributed physical fulfillment.

Rather than treating store operations as isolated manual tasks, I approach Shopify Plus, 3PL Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), and helpdesk integrations as an interconnected distributed state machine. Whether resolving asynchronous race conditions, containing multi-node freight surcharges, eliminating margin-eroding discount collisions, or authoring mission-critical Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), my focus is absolute operational resilience, zero data corruption, and deterministic predictability.
